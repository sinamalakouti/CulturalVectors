## Overview

We study **cultural vectors** as controllable representations of cultural knowledge in text-to-image diffusion models. We analyze their learning from synthetic and real data, inference-time scaling, representation in the U-Net and activation space, and compositionality for multi-cultural and cross-cultural generation.
